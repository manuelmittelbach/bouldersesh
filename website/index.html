How it works

Three taps from your couch to the wall.

No feeds to scroll for hours, no swiping. Just the sessions happening near you, today and tomorrow.

STEP 01

Find a session

Pick your city and gym. See who's climbing, when, and at what level — beginner to crusher.

STEP 02

Or start your own

Choose a gym, set a time, open a few spots. Your session goes live to boulderers in your city.

STEP 03

Climb together

Everyone in the session lands in one chat to sort the details. It disappears afterwards — no clutter.
